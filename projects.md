## Cyclistic Bike-Share Case Study


**Business Objective:** 
Analyze behavioral differences between casual riders and annual members to support strategies aimed at increasing membership conversion.


**Tools & Skills**  
R · Tableau · Data Cleaning · Data Visualization · Storytelling


**Key Analysis Focus**
- Differences in usage patterns between casual riders and members  
- Temporal trends (weekdays vs weekends, seasonality)  
- Behavioral indicators that suggest conversion opportunities  

**Outcome**  
This analysis translates rider behavior and usage patterns into evidence-based insights, supporting marketing decisions aimed at increasing annual membership conversion and long-term customer retention.
